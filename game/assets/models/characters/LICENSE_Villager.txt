Villager.glb

The game owner's own asset: a low-poly bearded villager generated by the owner with an AI 3D
generator and supplied for this game. All rights with the game owner; used here with permission.

Changes: tools/rig/rig_villager.py (reproducible, the 26 MB source is not in the repo):
decimated to ~13k tris with flat shading, textures reduced to one 1024 JPEG base colour, hair
and trouser texels made neutral so villagers can dye them, clothes added (linen shirt, leather
vest, apron), rigged on the KayKit Adventurers skeleton with its animations (CC0, see
LICENSE_KayKit.txt), exported as GLB.

Woman.glb and Elder.glb (the hamlet's human cast: Maren, Hester, Old Bram) are derived from the
same owner's asset by tools/rig/rig_cast.py: the woman's figure, beardless face, hair and
clothes (blouse, bodice, skirt, apron, bun, headscarf, braid) and the elder's knitted jumper and
wool cap are generated by the script from the villager's own mesh and texture; rigged on the
KayKit skeleton the same way. Same terms as above (owner's asset, used with permission).
