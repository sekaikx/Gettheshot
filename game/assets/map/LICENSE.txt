Famiglia country map plate: country_map.png, country_map_small.png, map_lines.json
=================================================================================

Baked by game/tools/map/build_map.py (original code, part of this project). The artwork itself is
original to this project; the data it was drawn from is all public domain:

- Natural Earth 1:10m vector data (coastline, land, minor islands, lakes, rivers and lake
  centrelines, admin-0 countries and boundary lines, admin-1 states/provinces and their lines,
  marine and region polygons). Public domain. https://www.naturalearthdata.com
  (downloaded from https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector)
- Relief hachures and the 100-fathom line: AWS Terrain Tiles ("terrarium", zoom 6), which at that
  zoom are built only from USGS GMTED2010 and NOAA ETOPO1 - both US-government works in the
  public domain. https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/
- City, route and rail coordinates: real latitudes/longitudes (facts), from the game design brief.

No map publisher's plate, logo or lettering was copied; "The Famiglia Atlas" is a made-up imprint.
